# AI Collaboration Disclosure

This repository documents the token/KV-cache eviction research program and
accompanying manuscript, developed through an integrated human–AI research
workflow. This file records how that collaboration worked and where
responsibility for the final scientific work sits.

## Elias De Jesús — Research direction and scientific responsibility

Elias De Jesús selected and directed the research problem, formulated and
evaluated the hypotheses tested throughout the research program, made the
decisions about research direction and stopping criteria at each stage,
interpreted and accepted or rejected the resulting conclusions, reviewed
the mathematical and computational results, and assumes responsibility for
the final scientific claims and the manuscript.

## Claude (Anthropic) — Repository implementation and computational research

Claude contributed extensively to repository-based implementation:
experiment construction and execution, test development, computational and
statistical auditing (including the cluster-aware statistical reanalysis),
literature-assisted novelty checking, figure generation, LaTeX manuscript
development, reproducibility auditing, and repository preparation.

## ChatGPT (OpenAI) — Research reasoning, synthesis, and critical evaluation

ChatGPT contributed to research planning, mathematical and conceptual
analysis, hypothesis development and refinement, interpretation of
experimental rounds, identification of follow-up tests and falsification
strategies, critical evaluation of claims and overclaim risk, manuscript
positioning and structure, and synthesis of the overall research program.

## Responsibility statement

Both AI systems functioned as interactive computational and reasoning
collaborators/tools throughout this project. Neither Claude nor ChatGPT
independently conducted or approved this research, and neither system can
assume scholarly responsibility for it. Elias De Jesús reviewed the outputs
of both systems and retains responsibility for the published work.
